Royal Beets

Taking beets to a whole new level

Royal Beets started when Nyk Regine, an aspiring chef, was diagnosed with chronic iron deficiency anemia and went looking for food that would help.

Beets kept coming up in her research, but she couldn't stand the pickled kind. Then she realized the beets weren't the problem. The pickling was. So she started cooking her own.

Nearly twenty years of recipes later, those experiments fill the shelves at Corner Market. Everything is handcrafted and slow-cooked in small batches, from relish for your hot dog to beet truffles and cookies.

A Lincolnville corner with a long memory

Before it was a market, this building served the neighborhood as a public library, a barbershop and a pool hall.

Lincolnville is one of St. Augustine's oldest Black neighborhoods, and the market is meant to be a place to sit down and stay a while.
